Good to know

Questions dog owners ask

What exactly is a puppuccino?

A frozen pup cup — a dog-sized frozen treat served in a cup — built to order at the bar and customized the way your pup likes it.

What can I buy in the shop?

Treats, training tools, harnesses, leashes, toys, interactive toys, bowls, plants and 3D-printed items — the retail side is stocked with the goods owners actually run out of.

Are humans welcome too?

Yes. The shop is canine forward, and drinks and snacks for people are in the works.

Where exactly are you?

5802 E Sprague Ave, Spokane Valley, WA 99212 — the same suite where the owner runs the Confident K9 Training dog-training business.

How do I check what's on today?

Call (509) 768-1353 or message the shop on Facebook — that's where the day's specials get posted.
