The treat

What a puppuccino is

A puppuccino is a pup cup — a dog-sized frozen treat served in a cup — and here it isn't off the menu, it's built your dog's way. The bar at Puppuccino is fully customizable, so the cup that lands in front of your pup is the one you ordered for them.

The shop is canine forward: these cups are made for dogs, the guests who get the counter. Drinks and snacks for their people are in the works.

Where to pick one up

Puppuccino sits at 5802 E Sprague Ave, Spokane Valley, WA 99212 — the same suite where the owner runs Confident K9 Training — one owner, both businesses, one door on Sprague. Grab a pup cup before a training session, after one, or on the way through the Valley.

Not just a treat shop

The shelves beside the bar carry treats, toys, bowls and training gear — see the pet shop — and the shop has hosted Trunk-or-Treat parties for the local pack, so watch Facebook for the next event.
