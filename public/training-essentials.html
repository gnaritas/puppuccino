The training wall

Tools, harnesses and leashes — chosen by a trainer

The training tools, harnesses and leashes at Puppuccino come from the same hands that run Confident K9 Training in the same suite at 5802 E Sprague Ave. When the person stocking the wall is the person training the dogs, the gear tends to be the stuff that actually works.

The pup cup as a reward

Training runs on good rewards, and a frozen pup cup built to order makes a memorable one. Ask the counter how the regulars put their puppuccinos to use after a session next door — the shop and the training business share a door, a suite and a client list.

While you're here

The rest of the racks: treats, toys, interactive toys, bowls, plants and 3D-printed finds — the full shelf list is on the pet shop page.
